# CIA Triad in Cybersecurity

## Objective
To understand the three basic principles of information security.

## What is the CIA Triad?
The CIA Triad is a security model based on three important principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

## 1. Confidentiality
Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized people.

Example:
Using passwords and encryption to protect personal information.

## 2. Integrity
Ensures that information remains accurate and is not changed without authorization.

Example:
Using file hashes to check whether a file has been modified.

## 3. Availability
Ensures that information and systems are accessible when authorized users need them.

Example:
Maintaining backups and reliable servers.

## Importance
- Protects sensitive information.
- Prevents unauthorized changes.
- Keeps systems and services accessible.
- Supports secure information management.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understanding the CIA Triad.
- Learning the three main principles of information security.
- Identifying real-world security examples.

## Result
Learned the fundamental principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
