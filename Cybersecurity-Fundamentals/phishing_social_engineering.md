# Phishing and Social Engineering

## Objective
To understand phishing attacks and social engineering techniques.

## What is Phishing?
Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers use fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

## Common Types of Phishing
1. Email Phishing – Fraudulent emails that imitate trusted organizations.
2. Spear Phishing – Targeted phishing aimed at a particular person or organization.
3. Smishing – Phishing through SMS messages.
4. Vishing – Phishing through phone calls.
5. Whaling – Targeted phishing against senior executives or important individuals.

## What is Social Engineering?
Social engineering is the manipulation of people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that may compromise security.

## Common Warning Signs
- Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
- Suspicious links or attachments.
- Unusual sender addresses.
- Urgent requests for payments or account verification.
- Unexpected requests for passwords or OTPs.

## Prevention Methods
- Verify the sender before responding.
- Avoid opening suspicious links and attachments.
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Keep software updated.
- Report suspicious messages.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understanding phishing and social engineering.
- Identifying common warning signs.
- Learning basic prevention methods.

## Result
Learned how phishing and social engineering work and how to recognize suspicious messages.
