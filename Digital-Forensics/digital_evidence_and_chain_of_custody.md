# Digital Evidence and Chain of Custody

## 1. What is Digital Evidence?
Digital evidence is information stored or transmitted in digital form that may help in an investigation.

## 2. Examples of Digital Evidence
- Documents and images
- Emails and messages
- System logs
- Browser history
- Network records
- File metadata

## 3. What is Chain of Custody?
Chain of custody is the documented record of how digital evidence is collected, handled, transferred, and stored.

## 4. Importance
- Maintains evidence integrity.
- Records who handled the evidence.
- Helps identify unauthorized changes.
- Supports reliable investigations.

## 5. Evidence Handling Process
1. Identify the evidence.
2. Record its source and details.
3. Preserve the original evidence.
4. Create a working copy when appropriate.
5. Record every handling or transfer.
6. Store evidence securely.

## 6. Best Practices
- Obtain proper authorization.
- Avoid modifying original evidence.
- Record dates, times, and actions.
- Use file hashes to check integrity.
- Keep investigation records clear.

## Conclusion
Proper evidence handling and chain of custody help make digital forensic investigations reliable and trustworthy.
