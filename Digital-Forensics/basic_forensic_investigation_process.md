# Basic Forensic Investigation Process

## 1. Introduction
A digital forensic investigation is a structured process used to examine digital evidence and understand a security incident.

## 2. Investigation Steps

### Step 1: Preparation
- Define the investigation purpose.
- Obtain proper authorization.
- Identify the required tools.

### Step 2: Identification
- Identify possible sources of digital evidence.
- Note relevant devices, files, and logs.

### Step 3: Preservation
- Protect original evidence from modification.
- Record evidence details.
- Maintain chain of custody.

### Step 4: Collection
- Collect evidence using appropriate forensic methods.
- Document the collection process.

### Step 5: Examination
- Examine collected evidence.
- Review relevant files, metadata, and logs.

### Step 6: Analysis
- Identify relevant findings.
- Organize information into a timeline.
- Look for patterns related to the incident.

### Step 7: Reporting
- Document the methods used.
- Present findings clearly.
- Mention limitations and conclusions.

## 3. Important Principles
- Work only with proper authorization.
- Preserve original evidence.
- Maintain accurate records.
- Verify file integrity using hashes.
- Keep findings objective.

## 4. Conclusion
Following a structured investigation process helps ensure that digital evidence is handled carefully and findings are reliable.
